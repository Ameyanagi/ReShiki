# Drawing tools, geometry and floating NMR (integration under review)

Projected cages previously lost visible rim ink when fading used a depth midplane. Mechanism curves shared one bend control, pen continuation could lose its selection, and geometry/default controls required repeated manual adjustment. This integration brings the reviewed fixes together on the retained MOL XYZ/implicit-H and dual EMF import base.

## Drawing behavior

Rear opacity applies to view-occluded cage ink while leaving the full exposed outline solid. Automatic double rails use a bounded local XYZ face/tangent calculation. Closed-surface validation and geometry/query budgets are conservative heuristics; open, invalid or over-budget inputs fall back to opaque visibility, while missing/unusable projection geometry keeps the established rail fallback. This is not a formal per-pixel certificate for every molecule. Setting opacity alone preserves chemistry and coordinates; a 3D tilt rotates XYZ and therefore changes coordinate values.

Curved mechanism arrows have independent end tangents. Moving an attached target carries its endpoint and adjacent control once; editing an endpoint detaches it. New atom/bond/lone-pair picks use actually surviving ink, preserving implicit carbon targeting where visible front/rim incident bonds remain. Completely hidden rear ink cannot acquire a new attachment, but existing links remain valid. Opaque and remote-pointer queries keep the extra visibility caches cold. The linked historical guide records checkbox/Alt free placement, source-click cancellation and external attachment-loss notices.

Connected pen paths retain straight/cubic segments, adjacent controls and their affine/depth frame through node edits and Continue. Reference alignment sets a selected edge to horizontal, vertical or a chosen angle; quarter-turn copies share one pin. Stretch varies one bridge length along its original axis and translates one branch rigidly. A ring bridge or a branch sharing a derived attachment with fixed atoms is refused. Porphine is the defined free-base 21H,23H core, not a general porphyrin generator.

Clicked lone pairs use visible owner/neighbor clearance; explicitly dragged offsets are retained. Orbital atom snapping is independent of chemical attachment, with Alt/Option bypass and consistent preview/commit behavior. Label clearance is transparent and respects opacity; it does not paint a white halo or move chemistry.

The approved NMR panel floats or docks with the same report. Atom-number, ppm or Both labels follow their original owners; equal-height sticks use actual values on a descending ppm axis. Labels and selection halos remain session overlays outside native documents, exported figures and molecular occlusion surfaces. Chemistry edits clear stale results, per-tab/background ownership is retained, and palette operations preserve camera and history. These are predicted shifts, not J coupling, multiplets, second-order simulation or integrated intensity. Supported neutral closed-shell organic graphs use the bundled measured CDCl₃ reference conditions; sparse/unsupported sites report limits. Conditions and stereoisomers are pooled; proton rows describe attached-H groups, not stereospecific individual protons. [Method and data](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/blob/828dcd192aa1146de7e8d23b5d9dbebf26af0763/docs/nmr-prediction.md).
