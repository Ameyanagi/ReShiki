# Centered double bonds meet their neighbours

A centered double bond at an unlabeled carbon, such as the C=O of acetone or
cyclohexanone, used to stop each line just short of the neighbouring single
bonds, leaving a small notch at the carbon. Each line now runs on until it
meets the single bond on its side, as in ChemDraw, without passing that bond's
outer edge. Canvas and SVG/PNG/PDF export share the change.

Unchanged: labeled ends (the O), ends with no other bond, neighbours that are
centered doubles or triples (cumulenes), wedge, hashed or bold neighbours, and
angles where the meeting point would be far from the carbon. Bond highlights
grow to cover the joined corners.
