- After five exports or picture copies, on a later day than the first launch,
  the desktop app asks once in a small card at the bottom right of the canvas:
  **Enjoying ReShiki?** It waits until you have not touched the window for 30
  seconds and never appears over a menu or dialog. **Star on GitHub** opens the
  project page in your browser. After **Not now**, it asks again at most once,
  in a later feature release and at least 90 days later. Nothing is sent
  anywhere; see the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md#github-star-request).
- **ReShiki → Check for updates** has a **Star on GitHub ↗** link.
