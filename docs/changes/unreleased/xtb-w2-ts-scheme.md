- Select two or more consecutive reaction arrows and choose **Calculate scheme…**. Each step starts with reaction energy and barrier selected. Free energies at 298 K can be selected separately for the reaction and the barrier. Shared species use one optimized structure.
- The calculation adds a missing small molecule or a second copy of a drawn species when one choice balances the step exactly. The sheet names the addition. If there is no unique match, it explains the imbalance and lets you continue.
- The **Path** tab shows the energy change for each step and for the whole scheme. Retry a failed step individually or cancel and keep finished results. **Place energy profile** adds the linked figure to the drawing. Playback follows each intermediate and transition state.
- Energy profile playback advances one state at a time when Reduce Motion is on in macOS or the browser.
