- **Place energy profile** on a reaction result or in ⌘K adds an energy diagram below the scheme and its 3D models. The diagram shows the reaction energy, the verified transition state when available, and its IRC curve when the path connects the drawn endpoints. Compound labels follow the reaction card.
- The diagram follows reruns, g-xTB refinement, and changes to the energy unit. Use **Unlink** to freeze the current figure. The Properties panel can put the 3D models beside their energy levels, in a row above, or hide them.
- Energy profiles save with the drawing and export to PDF, SVG, and PNG. Copying the whole scheme keeps the diagram linked to the pasted reaction; a diagram copied on its own pastes as a static picture.
