- **Add joint…** defines a fixed ground-glass socket or cone from two rim edges in an equipment draft. Choose a standard size, review the generated joint and outward arrow, then add it to the draft. The first joint sets the piece's scale. Draft Undo stays separate from the drawing, and Accept remains one drawing edit. Standard joints require a compatible filled glass neck; liquid, moving-part and library authoring remain for later slices.
