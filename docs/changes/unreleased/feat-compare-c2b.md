- **Compare** matches a molecule that was both edited and given new ids, as
  after importing a scheme again, by its structure: adding a nitro group to a
  re-imported ester is one chemical line, **NO₂ added**, painted only where
  it changed and marked **matched by structure (ids changed)**, instead of a
  molecule removed and another added. A stereo flip drawn anew is matched by
  its constitution. Unrelated molecules, small ones and unclear pairs still
  show as removed and added. See [GitHub](github.md).
