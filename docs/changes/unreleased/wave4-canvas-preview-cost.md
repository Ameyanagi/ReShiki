---

- Ring, template and bond previews and moves of part of a structure stay
  quick in large drawings. A preview now redraws only the structures it
  changes, so its cost no longer grows with the rest of the drawing.
- Panning a zoomed-out drawing no longer redraws every bond and label on each
  frame. The first pan at a zoom level prepares the drawing a little beyond
  the window's edges, and later pans move that drawing.
