- **Compare** in History, and the **changed on GitHub** dialog, now tell
  chemistry from layout. Moving a molecule, or the whole scheme, is one
  **Moved** line instead of painting every atom; only real chemical edits are
  painted. A badge says **Chemically identical**, **1 chemical change** or
  **Couldn't verify all chemistry**; **Chemistry**, **Layout** and
  **Everything** filter the marks and the list, with a count on each; **Line
  up moved molecules** shows them where the other version has them; and
  **What changed** lists each change with its SMILES before → after. The
  drawing you have open is on the right, framed in green as **CURRENT**; the
  version you compare with is on the left, framed in dashed grey as
  **COMPARED**. See
  [GitHub](github.md).
