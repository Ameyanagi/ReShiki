- xTB **Vibrations** opens paused on the strongest IR band, or on an imaginary
  mode when there is one. The mode list shows wavenumber, IR intensity in
  km/mol and the motion (C=O stretch, O–H stretch, ring stretch, H–C–H bend,
  out-of-plane bend, torsion), sorted strongest first or by wavenumber.
  Clicking a peak selects its mode.
- The xTB results palette at the bottom takes at most 40 % of the window and
  opens collapsed in windows shorter than 800 pt; a failed run stays open so its
  retry buttons show. Chart labels are easier to read in dark mode, and the
  Steps chart names its axes.
- Exports from the results palette name the saved file, and its messages clear
  when you close the palette or switch documents.
