- The **ATOMS** buttons and **Help** stay in view at the bottom of the left
  palette at every window size. Only the tools scroll, and they fade out at the
  bottom when more are below. At 1280 × 848, all 11 atom buttons now show
  instead of H, C and N only.
- **ATOMS** has its own line for **\*** (dummy atom) and a new **R** button,
  which draws an R group: a `*` atom labeled R. **Fe** left the palette; type
  `Fe` in the Atom tool's symbol field.
- Tools are paired by family: Select and Lasso, Eraser and 3D tilt, the bond
  tools, Atom and Ring, the two chains, Arrow and Text, then the drawing tools.
  Each row is a little shorter.
- The right panel keeps one width, 320 px, on every tab, so switching tabs no
  longer moves the tabs or the drawing. The tabs are a segmented control with
  12 px labels.
- Panel actions such as **Copy SMILES**, **Predict NMR…** and **Group** look
  like buttons. **Copy SMILES** and **Copy analysis** sit side by side, after
  the SMILES.
- In Properties, a chemistry error shows under the summary at the top, and
  **Delete selection** is the last button in the panel. With a selection,
  **Molecular properties** no longer repeats the atom and bond counts.
- Shorter panel text: the stereo group legend, the low-contrast advice, the
  page setup notes and the advice in the PubChem confirmation moved into
  hints.
- The **View** bar lists canvas aids first and data display after them. On a
  narrow window it wraps to a second line instead of hiding **Page setup…** and
  **Done**.
