- **Compare** tells a flipped or scaled molecule, shape or scheme from a
  redraw, with **MIRRORED** and **SCALED** lines (**Molecule flipped
  left–right**, **Molecule scaled to 120%**). A molecule flipped with the Flip
  tool keeps its configuration and is not a chemical change; a mirror image
  drawn without swapping wedges reads **Mirror image: every stereocentre
  inverted**. Chemical lines now say what changed with the drawing's own atom
  numbers, such as **OH added at C4** or **Cl → Br at C2**. See
  [GitHub](github.md).
