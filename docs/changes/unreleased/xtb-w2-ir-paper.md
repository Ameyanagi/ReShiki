- The placed and exported IR spectrum is now publication-style:
  transmittance with bands pointing down (or absorbance), 4000 → 400 cm⁻¹,
  a framed plot with inward ticks, journal fonts and line widths, one- or
  two-column size, and peak labels that avoid each other. Selecting the
  placed figure shows its settings in Properties; every change is one Undo
  step, each peak label can be switched off, and "Copy caption" copies a
  ready-made figure caption.
