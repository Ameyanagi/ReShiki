- **Place MO diagram** (Orbitals tab or ⌘K) adds the molecule's orbital energy diagram to the drawing as one object:
  - an eV axis and the levels from HOMO−2 to LUMO+2, with degenerate levels side by side;
  - electrons on the occupied levels, the HOMO and LUMO named, and the gap marked;
  - α and β columns for open shells;
  - small orbital pictures beside the levels you choose (HOMO and LUMO at first); click a level to add or remove one.

  It follows a new calculation of the same molecule in place, unless you unlink it. It saves, copies, prints and exports with the drawing.
- Saved orbital pictures now survive generic (Markush) expansion and Move & attach. Before, they could vanish where meshes cannot be rebuilt, such as the browser.
