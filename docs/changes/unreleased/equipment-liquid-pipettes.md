- The equipment catalogue now includes Komagome pipettes in 1, 2, 3, 5 and 10 mL, short and long Pasteur pipettes, single-mark volumetric pipettes in 1, 2, 5, 10 and 25 mL, and intermediate-mark and tip-graduated measuring pipettes in 1, 2, 5 and 10 mL. Droppers have a fitted/removable rubber bulb control. All have adjustable drawn liquid levels and colors; the illustrations and scale marks are schematic rather than calibrated measuring instruments.
- An electric pipette controller is available for glass volumetric and graduated pipettes, with two finger buttons for aspiration and dispensing and an adapter-section view of its silicone mount.
- A blue rubber safety pipette filler is available with standard and small-bore-adapter appearances for manual filling of glass pipettes.
