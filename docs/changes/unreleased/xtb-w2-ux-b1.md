- In builds with xTB, the row above the canvas offers **3D model ▾**: **Optimize
  with xTB (quantum, GFN2)** first, then **Opt + freq (xTB)**, the force-field
  preview (now named **Force-field 3D model (MMFF94s)…**) and **More xTB
  settings…**.
- The command palette finds xTB calculations by what they give: geometry,
  minimize, energy, frequencies, IR, HOMO, LUMO, orbitals, conformers, quantum
  or DFT.
- **Predict** reopens on the page you last used, so it no longer starts an NMR
  prediction while you work with xTB, and NMR messages stay off the xTB card.
  Each xTB method says what it is for, and **Run** stays at the foot of the
  inspector while **Advanced** is open.
- With nothing or several molecules selected, the xTB card says which and asks
  you to click one; the hint clears once you do.
