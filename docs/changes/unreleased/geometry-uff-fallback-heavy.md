- 3D models now build molecules MMFF94s cannot type, such as O₂, ozone and CO, with UFF instead of stopping, and say so in the status bar.
- The 3D model view now shows radicals and compounds of elements such as cerium, uranium, thorium and xenon as approximate UFF models with a note, where it used to refuse them. Six-coordinate drawings such as UF₆ now come out octahedral; before, two fluorines collapsed to 1.19 Å apart.
