# Unreleased changes

- **Under review — 3D models: rotate, resize and the Show-as bar:** once a shown model is selected, drag inside its frame to rotate it, **Shift**-drag to roll it and double-click to reset the view; corner handles resize the frame. An unselected model, or its frame's edge, still drags to move. A small **Show as 2D · 3D · Both** bar floats above a selected molecule and hides while you drag. Going back to 2D can keep the **Original drawing** or flatten the model **From the 3D view** as it is turned now, with far bonds lighter, like 3D optimize. When a model appears, its atoms move from the drawing into 3D and the hydrogens grow out. Each rotation, resize and flatten is one Undo step, and every drag commits exactly what it previews. [3D models](3d-keyboard-drawing.md#show-a-molecule-as-a-3d-model) · [Issue #386](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/issues/386) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Under review — 3D models of structures:** select a molecule and choose **2D**, **3D** or **Both** in the new **Properties → Structure** section to show it as a vector ball & stick model in place of its drawing or beside it, with the shading, outlines, depth fade and perspective of the approved prototype. The conformer comes from the 3D optimize worker (ETKDG, then MMFF94s) and is kept for switching back; each switch is one Undo step, and chemistry, atom IDs and the 2D layout never change. Clicking the model selects its molecule; a chemical edit removes the model. `.rsk` files keep models, and PNG, SVG and PDF draw them. Every file is now saved as document version 28, so ReShiki 0.12.0 cannot open it. [3D models](3d-keyboard-drawing.md#show-a-molecule-as-a-3d-model) · [Issue #386](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/issues/386) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Under review — Smaller circled charges:** a circled charge (⊕/⊖) on an atom now draws with a circle 0.80 × its mark size across and a sign 0.48 × the size (6.0 pt and 3.6 pt at ACS's 7.5 pt), so it no longer stands taller than an O. A charge drawn in ReShiki uses the same nominal size as one from ChemDraw (0.75 × the label font), so both look the same, and charges in existing documents also draw smaller. The stored size is unchanged, so CDXML and clipboard round-trips with ChemDraw keep the same charge size; plain + and − and free circled symbols keep their size. The canvas, hit-testing, label clearance and SVG, PDF and PNG exports all use the new circle. [Issue #388](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/issues/388) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Under review — 3D tilt keeps turning:** a 3D tilt drag no longer stops at 75°; the selection keeps rotating for as long as the drag continues (0.5° per pixel, past 90° and 180° and all the way round), Shift still snaps to 15°, and the live readout shows the angle as −180…+180°. The preview matches the result, which is one Undo step, and a full turn restores the original positions. **Tilt X / Tilt Y** in the numeric transform panel accept −36000…36000° like **Rotate** instead of ±85°. [Numeric transforms](changes/numeric-transforms.md) · [Issue #389](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/issues/389) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Under review — Label alignment survives ChemDraw:** **Flush left** and **Centered** text labels (MeO) and explicit group alignments (Boc) now come back unchanged after copying to ChemDraw and back, instead of being laid out again as Automatic. Copies now also write the atom's label display, which is where ChemDraw keeps its **Text** alignment choice, and pasting a **Centered** label from ChemDraw no longer fails. A flush-right MeO returns as a flush-right methoxy group, as ChemDraw reads it that way. [Atom labels](atom-labels.md) · [Issue #378](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/issues/378) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Under review — Clear message for salt names:** **Import → Name** with a salt or mixture such as `sodium chloride` or `sodium acetate` now says "Salts and mixtures aren't supported yet. Draw the ions, or paste SMILES under Import → Structure." instead of "Unsupported or ambiguous name. Try a more specific name." Salts are still not imported from names; other rejected names keep their message. [Chemical names](chemical-naming-rust.md#limits) · [Issue #380](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/issues/380) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Under review — Typed reverse spellings and group names:** a group's reverse spelling typed on a bond end now makes that group: **MeO** gives OMe, **TBSO** gives OTBS, **O2N** gives NO2 and **(HO)2B** gives B(OH)2, each shown in whichever spelling suits the free side. **NC** and **OCN** are read from the bond: on a left-hand end they give CN and NCO, while on a right-hand end, where they would mean an isocyanide or a cyanate, they stay text and the editor says why. A full name such as **morpholino**, **2-thienyl** or **acetyl** stays text, and the label editor suggests the label to type ("Did you mean Morph?", "Did you mean Ac or COMe?"). Names with locants, such as 2-thienyl and propan-2-yl, no longer give the link-node error "A link node requires a finite number range". [Typing groups](abbreviations.md#chemical-abbreviations) · [Issue #376](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/issues/376) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Under review — Aimed curved arrows keep their head off bonds:** after you drag the end tangent of an attached curved arrow, its head now stays clear of bond lines, as a newly drawn arrow's does. Your aim and the attachment are kept; the end only moves around the atom to the side you aimed from. If you aim straight along a bond, the head keeps your aim. [Issue #379](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/issues/379) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Under review — COSMolKit 0.5.0:** InChI generation and import, and 3D generation, relaxation and energies with MMFF94, MMFF94s and UFF, now use COSMolKit 0.5.0 instead of a patched 0.3.0 branch, since 0.5.0 includes those fixes. Generated 3D starting geometries for a given seed can differ from earlier versions; the same checks on stereochemistry, cages and fixed atoms still apply · [Issue #369](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/issues/369) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Under review — Compact command palette:** results are one line each (name with the matched letters in bold, category and shortcut), with the selected command's description below the list, so about ten fit instead of six. Category chips with counts, clicked or cycled with **Tab** / **Shift+Tab**, narrow broad searches such as **ring**; commands that cannot run now sort below the rest, name matches rank above keyword matches, and **Page Up/Page Down**, **Home** and **End** move through the list. [Shortcuts guide](contextual-shortcuts.md#run-a-command) · [Issue #372](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/issues/372) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Merged — Nix flake:** build and install ReShiki with `nix run github:Ameyanagi/ReShiki` on Linux (x64 and ARM) and Apple Silicon Macs. The package contains the single `reshiki` executable (on a Mac inside `ReShiki.app`, which nix-darwin and home-manager add to Applications), runs an InChI and PNG export check after building, and is built in CI whenever the flake or Cargo manifests change. [PR #366](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/366) · @Stefanomarton.

- **Merged — 297 more abbreviations:** the catalog now holds 359 groups, including protecting groups (Alloc, Teoc, SEM, THP, DMTr, Mtt, Pbf, Dde, ivDde, Acm), carbonyl and acid derivatives (CHO, CO2tBu, CONMe2, Weinreb amide), sulfonyl and sulfur groups (Ns, SO2Ph, SO3H, NTf2), phosphorus, boron, tin and zinc groups (PPh2, P(O)(OEt)2, Bpin, B(OH)2, SnBu3, ZnCl), amines (NMe2, Morph) and aryl and heteroaryl rings (Mes, Dipp, PMP, 2-Py, 2-Thienyl). Every group's formula was written independently and checked against its SMILES, and every label is tested by typing it on a bond end. Skeleton groups such as rings, amines and amides are typed only; the new protecting and reagent groups are detected, so **Contract common groups** can now prefer a larger group such as NHCbz, OTMS or SO2Ph. [Group list](abbreviations.md#chemical-abbreviations) · [PR #364](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/364) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Merged — Your own abbreviations:** select a group with one bond to the rest of the drawing, enter a label and choose **Define abbreviation from selection** to save it. Typing that label on any endpoint then creates the real group, and **Contract common groups** finds it. Groups are stored in `abbreviations.json` in the data folder in the bundled catalog format; entries that cannot be used are skipped with a status message naming the problem. [Your own groups](abbreviations.md#your-own-groups) · [PR #363](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/363) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Merged — One editable abbreviation catalog:** every chemical abbreviation now comes from one catalog file of labels, SMILES, formulas, categories and full names. Matching rules, formulas and layouts are derived from each SMILES with ReShiki's own depiction, so adding a group is one entry and the per-platform geometry captures are retired. The 62 existing groups keep identical layouts. Aliases such as TBDMS, MPM and Trt now create their groups. [Catalog format](abbreviations.md#the-group-catalog) · [PR #362](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/362) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Merged — Protecting-group abbreviations:** typing common carbohydrate and protecting-group labels such as OBn, OTBS, OPMB, NHAc, NHBoc, OTr, OTf or the trichloroacetimidate OC(NH)CCl3 on an endpoint now creates the real group instead of text, so formula and mass match the expanded structure. Thirty-one presets were added with captured geometry for every supported platform. [PR #348](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/348) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Merged — Command palette:** press **:** to search every command (insertions such as benzene, OMe or bromine; bond and atom edits such as double bond; menu, panel and tool actions such as Predict NMR, export formats and zoom) and run it with Enter on the selection or the pointed atom or bond, as one Undo step. Rows show their shortcuts; vim-style **:w**, **:q**, **:wq** and others run file commands. Stacked on the shortcut finder. [Shortcuts guide](contextual-shortcuts.md#run-a-command) · [PR #361](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/361) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Merged — Generic labels sit beside their bond:** an alternative list such as `[Cl,Br,I]` with a single horizontal bond now meets the bond with its near edge, so the text extends away from the molecule instead of being centered over the bond. Vertical alternative lists and internal generic labels, including link nodes, stay centered. [Atom labels](atom-labels.md) · [PR #365](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/365) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Merged — Bonds meet the attachment atom in text labels:** in multi-atom labels such as MeO, AcS or SPh on a sugar's anomeric carbon, the bond now meets the O or S instead of the middle of the label; nicknames such as Me, Ac and Ph never take the bond. A new **Bond attachment (text labels)** setting chooses flush left, centered, flush right or a specific character; the first three round-trip through ChemDraw copy and paste as label justification. [Atom labels](atom-labels.md) · [PR #360](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/360) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Merged — Shortcut finder:** Help (?, F1 or Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+/) is now a searchable list of every shortcut by action, key, description or synonym, showing where each applies, with live before/after drawings for atom and bond keys made by the keys' own edits. Selecting a row never runs the command. [Shortcuts guide](contextual-shortcuts.md) · [PR #357](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/357) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Merged — Cleaner crossings on 3D cages:** on projected drawings such as C60, bonds behind the cage are hidden wherever front bonds, double-bond rails, aromatic inner lines or ring circles cover them, at any crossing angle, along near-parallel overlaps and next to atoms, without leftover stubs, and front bonds stay whole, on the canvas and in SVG, PNG and PDF. [PR #356](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/356) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Merged — Arrow length follows its condition text:** text typed or dropped above or below a straight reaction arrow attaches to it, stays centred and keeps the arrow at least as long as the text plus padding. Editing fitted text resyncs the length, hand-lengthened arrows are kept, and dragging the text away detaches it. `.rsk` files keep the link. [Arrow conditions](arrows.md#write-conditions-on-an-arrow) · [PR #359](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/359) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Merged — Discoverable fine rotation:** dragging the rotation handle now shows the applied angle and the step keys (Shift for 15° steps, Alt/Option+Left/Right for 1°, Alt/Option+Up/Down for 15°), and a **°** field after Rotate 180° in the Select context row rotates the selection by a typed angle. Help and the selection tooltip list the same keys. [Selection handles](selection-transforms.md) · [PR #358](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/358) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Merged — Ring drag rotation:** drag with a ring tool to place its first atom at the press and turn the ring in 15° steps toward the pointer, from empty space or an atom; hold Option/Alt to rotate freely at the same size. [PR #355](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/355) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Merged — Atom text grows away from the drawing:** a longer custom atom number now starts beside its atom on the open side and is left- or right-justified, so extra text extends outward instead of being centred over bonds; automatic placement also avoids free captions. Short numbers and manually positioned indicators keep their positions. [PR #354](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/354) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Merged — Word-aware caption wrapping and `reshiki --version`:** wrapped text breaks only between words, or next to CJK characters, so generated name captions and `compose` labels such as `4-Bromoanisole` are never cut mid-word; a word wider than its box overflows it instead. `reshiki --version` and `reshiki --cli --version` print the version and exit without opening a window, and the Agent Skill and CLI guide tell agents to write formulas in labels and conditions with Unicode subscripts. [PR #343](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/343) · @Ameyanagi.

[Diagnostic evidence](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/actions/runs/38822804581) · [PR #328](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/328) · @Ameyanagi.
