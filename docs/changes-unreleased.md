# Unreleased changes

- **Under review — Command palette:** press **:** to search every command (insertions such as benzene, OMe or bromine; bond and atom edits such as double bond; menu, panel and tool actions such as Predict NMR, export formats and zoom) and run it with Enter on the selection or the pointed atom or bond, as one Undo step. Rows show their shortcuts; vim-style **:w**, **:q**, **:wq** and others run file commands. Stacked on the shortcut finder. [Shortcuts guide](contextual-shortcuts.md#run-a-command) · [PR #361](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/361) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Under review — Bonds meet the attachment atom in text labels:** in multi-atom labels such as MeO, AcS or SPh on a sugar's anomeric carbon, the bond now meets the O or S instead of the middle of the label; nicknames such as Me, Ac and Ph never take the bond. A new **Bond attachment (text labels)** setting chooses flush left, centered, flush right or a specific character; the first three round-trip through ChemDraw copy and paste as label justification. [Atom labels](atom-labels.md) · [PR #360](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/360) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Under review — Shortcut finder:** Help (?, F1 or Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+/) is now a searchable list of every shortcut by action, key, description or synonym, showing where each applies, with live before/after drawings for atom and bond keys made by the keys' own edits. Selecting a row never runs the command. [Shortcuts guide](contextual-shortcuts.md) · [PR #357](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/357) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Under review — Cleaner crossings on 3D cages:** on projected drawings such as C60, bonds behind the cage are hidden wherever front bonds, double-bond rails, aromatic inner lines or ring circles cover them, at any crossing angle, along near-parallel overlaps and next to atoms, without leftover stubs, and front bonds stay whole, on the canvas and in SVG, PNG and PDF. [PR #356](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/356) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Under review — Arrow length follows its condition text:** text typed or dropped above or below a straight reaction arrow attaches to it, stays centred and keeps the arrow at least as long as the text plus padding. Editing fitted text resyncs the length, hand-lengthened arrows are kept, and dragging the text away detaches it. `.rsk` files keep the link. [Arrow conditions](arrows.md#write-conditions-on-an-arrow) · [PR #359](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/359) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Under review — Discoverable fine rotation:** dragging the rotation handle now shows the applied angle and the step keys (Shift for 15° steps, Alt/Option+Left/Right for 1°, Alt/Option+Up/Down for 15°), and a **°** field after Rotate 180° in the Select context row rotates the selection by a typed angle. Help and the selection tooltip list the same keys. [Selection handles](selection-transforms.md) · [PR #358](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/358) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Under review — Ring drag rotation:** drag with a ring tool to place its first atom at the press and turn the ring in 15° steps toward the pointer, from empty space or an atom; hold Option/Alt to rotate freely at the same size. [PR #355](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/355) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Under review — Atom text grows away from the drawing:** a longer custom atom number now starts beside its atom on the open side and is left- or right-justified, so extra text extends outward instead of being centred over bonds; automatic placement also avoids free captions. Short numbers and manually positioned indicators keep their positions. [PR #354](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/354) · @Ameyanagi.
- **Under review — Word-aware caption wrapping and `reshiki --version`:** wrapped text breaks only between words, or next to CJK characters, so generated name captions and `compose` labels such as `4-Bromoanisole` are never cut mid-word; a word wider than its box overflows it instead. `reshiki --version` and `reshiki --cli --version` print the version and exit without opening a window, and the Agent Skill and CLI guide tell agents to write formulas in labels and conditions with Unicode subscripts. [PR #343](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/343) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Merged — Intel Mac panic recovery:** preserve the allocation alignment required by the native unwinder so recoverable background panics do not crash the application. Native Intel comparison reproduces the original crash and passes with the corrected alignment; global-allocator regression coverage now runs on all six supported native targets. [Diagnostic evidence](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/actions/runs/38822804581) · [PR #328](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/328) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Merged — Nightly Windows startup:** use Direct3D 12 consistently during automatic adapter discovery and renderer initialization, retaining explicit backend preferences and the Tiny Skia fallback. The candidate opens the editor in the Windows sandbox that reproduced the original startup failure. Installer antivirus detections remain under vendor review; this is not an antivirus-clearance claim. [Investigation and limits](release-0.12.0-windows-security.md) · [PR #327](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/327) · @Ameyanagi.

- **Under review — 0.12.0 release validation:** record the tagged source, the tag runs that did not publish, the publishing run, public-download checksums and macOS signing and package checks. [Validation record](release-0.12.0-validation.md) · [PR #324](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/pull/324) · @Ameyanagi.
