## From a first bond to a finished scheme

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Draw with precision" icon="pencil">
    Start with publication defaults, build chains and rings, and attach templates at an exact atom
    or bond. [Explore drawing tools](/moruno/reference/bond-tools/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keep every part editable" icon="document">
    Refine labels, arrows, pictures, and groups. Review cleanup on a selection and undo each applied
    change. [Work with selections](/moruno/reference/selection-and-groups/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Ask your assistant" icon="approve-check">
    Describe a molecule or reaction, review the proposed drawing, and apply it to the canvas with
    Undo available. [Use the assistant](/moruno/reference/assistant/)
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prepare a figure" icon="open-book">
    Use JACS / ACS styling, set publication pages, and export vector SVG or PDF and high-resolution
    PNG. [Prepare publication pages](/moruno/reference/publication-pages/)
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
