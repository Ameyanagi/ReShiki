## Write a caption

Choose **Text** (**t**, lowercase), click the canvas, and type. Double-click a caption to edit it. Select a text range to change its font, size, bold/italic, subscript, superscript, or color. **Done** or **Cmd/Ctrl+Enter** applies; **Escape** cancels.

## Number atoms

For atom numbers, hydrogen placement, and stereochemistry labels, use **Properties → Labels & chemistry → Atom labels & numbering…**. Select an atom to change charge, isotope, or unpaired electrons under **Atom details**.

Adding a charge to a skeletal carbon leaves the carbon label implicit. The charge remains visible at the vertex. Explicit carbon-label settings, isotope labels, and isolated atoms remain visible.

## Shorten a group

Select an atom and press **Enter** to edit its label. **Automatic** recognizes elements and defined groups; **Chemical abbreviation** chooses a nickname explicitly, including names that conflict with element symbols. **Text label** keeps literal text such as M, L or X on a wildcard atom. Text labels do not imply an element, query or molecular formula.

Use **Properties → Labels & chemistry → Chemical abbreviations…** to replace a terminal group with a preset such as **OMe**, or contract a selected fragment. **Expand selected** restores its atoms for editing.

Defined groups such as **Boc** retain their full atom/bond graph for composition. **Cp** is cyclopentadienyl (C5H5−); **Cp\*** is pentamethylcyclopentadienyl (C10H15−). Their attachment handles are not counted as atoms. Metal charge still needs the correct explicit assignment.

Group labels default to **Automatic** alignment. Select groups and use the existing top **Left / Center / Right** buttons; the adjacent menu restores **Automatic** or selects **Above**. Captions share those buttons, and mixed selections update together. Alignment never changes the group's atoms or bonds. Above positions one nickname above its anchor rather than stacking arbitrary formula tokens.

## Type formulas and explicit hydrogens

In the atom label editor, type a supported formula such as **C2H5** or **OCH3**. Automatic mode creates a chemical group with real atoms; its digits display as subscripts. Select several atoms and press **Enter** to give that fragment a group name. Use Text label only when you want literal, chemically unspecified text.

An explicit **NH3** label retains three hydrogens. At a metal, its donor contact can be represented without changing it into NH2. These explicit hydrogens participate in molecular properties; labels are not merely decorative. This does not turn off chemical validation.

Group and template definitions may be revised in future releases. [Open the shortcut examples](/guide/shortcuts/) to try the current groups in one editable document.

## Attach to several atoms

Select the target atoms, right-click → **Attachment points…**, and choose **Multi-center attachment** for simultaneous participation, such as η6-arene or η3-allyl. **Variable attachment** represents alternative attachment positions. Draw a bond from the new point to a metal or substituent. These are distinct from a plain dummy atom or drawing centroid.

Anonymous dummy and attachment handles remain visible in the editor but disappear from figures and image copies. Explicit labels remain visible. Native editing retains target IDs; supported editable exchange uses CDXML/CDX attachment nodes or V3000 ALL/ANY lists. See [the verified scope and limitations](/developer/chemdraw-attachment-comparison/).
