xTB orbitals come from a minimal basis. Use them to see the shape and node structure of frontier orbitals, not for quantitative orbital energies.

## What you will see

- **Orbitals** tab: a level diagram (occupied levels with ↑↓, HOMO and LUMO labelled, the gap marked) and a list of orbitals with their energies in eV. Click an orbital, or use ↑ and ↓, to choose one.
- The chosen orbital is drawn as a surface on the molecule's 3D model, in two phase colours. Controls: **Isovalue**, **Look** (Solid, Shaded, Outline, Mesh) and **Opacity**.
- The surface is a vector shape: it rotates with the model, hides correctly behind atoms and bonds, and exports sharp to SVG, PDF and EMF. Exported surfaces are simplified to about 20,000 triangles to keep files small.
- Orbitals of results read from Molden and Gaussian fchk files appear the same way.

## File size

Orbital coefficients and surfaces are not saved in the `.rsk` file. ReShiki recomputes them from the saved geometry and settings when you show an orbital (a fraction of a second for small molecules). Orbitals imported from other programs cannot be recomputed; they are kept when they take no more than 2 MB per molecule.
