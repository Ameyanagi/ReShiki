A conformer search looks for the low-energy shapes of a flexible molecule. Force-field energies alone often rank conformers in the wrong order, so ReShiki picks a diverse set with a force field and re-ranks it with xTB.

## Run a search

In **Predict → xTB**, choose the task **Conformers**, then set:

- **Starting structures**: how many random 3D structures ETKDG makes (default 100, up to 300). Seeds are fixed, so the same molecule gives the same result.
- **Minimize starts with**: MMFF94s (default), MMFF94, UFF, GFN-FF or GFN2-xTB. With GFN-FF or GFN2-xTB every start is minimized with that method and re-ranking is skipped; GFN2-xTB for every start is limited to 120 atoms and shows a time estimate.
- **Diversity**: how close two structures must be to share a cluster: fine (0.5 Å), normal (0.75 Å, default) or coarse (1.25 Å) heavy-atom RMSD, after alignment and taking symmetry into account.
- **Energy window**: starts more than this far above the lowest one (default 15 kcal/mol) are left out.
- **Re-rank up to** and **Per cluster**: how many cluster representatives xTB optimizes and ranks (default 20, up to 60), taking the lowest 1 or the lowest 3 of each cluster.

Click **Run**. The status shows the progress, for example `Embedding 100 · minimizing 64/100 (MMFF94s)`. **Cancel** keeps the conformers already re-ranked. The search is limited to 200 atoms, and to organic molecules the force fields can type.

The **Conformers** tab lists the kept conformers, lowest first:

- **ΔE xTB**, in kcal/mol, and the **ΔE** of the force field that minimized the starts (MMFF94s, MMFF94 or UFF), the **Boltzmann** population at 298.15 K, the **RMSD** to conformer 1, the **Cluster** size (how many starts fell into it) and a **Stereo** check against the drawing.
- Click a row to preview that conformer on the 3D model; Esc returns. **Overlay: All conformers** draws the others faintly behind it, aligned on the heavy atoms.
- **Use this conformer** puts it on the 3D model as one Undo step, after the same stereo check as an optimization.
- **Export XYZ…** and **Export SDF…** write the whole ensemble with energies and populations.

When the search finishes, the lowest conformer is applied to the 3D model after the same checks as an optimization (it waits for your choice if a stereocentre changed). Cancel during re-ranking keeps the conformers already finished. The file keeps up to 32 conformers in a compact form (about 16 KB for lysine).

You can also open an ensemble from another program, such as CREST's `crest_conformers.xyz`, and keep it in the Conformers tab; see [Open a file with several frames](/guide/calculation-files/#open-a-file-with-several-frames).
