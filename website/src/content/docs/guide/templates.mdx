## Macrocycles and ligands

The library includes 105 built-in templates. **Macrocycles** contains 12-crown-4, 15-crown-5, 18-crown-6, cyclen and cyclam. **Ligands** includes bipyridine, phenanthroline, terpyridine, triphenylphosphine, dppe, acetylacetone and ethylenediamine. Search or browse these collections, then use the same placement and attachment controls as other templates.

<Screenshot
  src="/doc-images/pr-reviews/pr28-templates.png"
  alt="The macrocycle and ligand collections as editable structures."
/>

These are the current group and template definitions. Their coverage, labels and layouts may be extended or revised in future releases. You can save your own editable copies and custom templates now.

[The shortcut reference](/guide/shortcuts/) includes one editable `.rsk` file of sample groups and structures to copy into your drawings.

## Draw carbohydrates

Choose **Carbohydrates** for α/β D-glucose, D-galactose, D-mannose, D-ribose and D-fructose Haworth templates. They contain defined stereochemistry and real atoms behind the **CH₂OH** labels. Search by sugar name, `Haworth`, `alpha` or `beta`, then place the template in empty space.

<Screenshot
  src="/doc-images/haworth/carbohydrate-templates.png"
  alt="Editable alpha and beta sugar Haworth projections beside the Carbohydrates library."
/>

The ring palette also offers **Haworth 5** and **Haworth 6** outlines. Oxygen-containing scaffolds are in Carbohydrates. Blank scaffolds do not assign stereochemistry automatically.

Native files, figure exports and checked CDXML/CDX exports retain the supplied Haworth projections' appearance. MOL and SMILES retain their defined chemistry. Editable export rejects a projection whose visible substituents conflict with its stored stereochemistry. [Read the Haworth usage and verification notes](/developer/haworth-projections/).

## Choose how it connects

Click the exact atom or bond in the large template preview, then its destination on the canvas.

| Mode                    | Result                                 |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| **Connect with a bond** | Joins two atoms with a new single bond |
| **Share an atom**       | Merges the chosen atoms                |
| **Fuse along a bond**   | Shares an edge and both endpoints      |

<Screenshot

/>

<Screenshot

/>

<Screenshot

Drag from the destination to choose a direction. **Shift/Ctrl** snaps the angle; **Alt/Option** frees it. Click empty space to place a separate structure.

**Keep placing** is on by default. Click successive atoms or bonds to attach more copies, including fused rings, without reselecting the template. Each placement is one Undo step. **Escape** ends placement; turn off **Keep placing** for a single insertion. Use **Browse templates**, your mouse's Back button, or **Alt+Left** to return to the library.

## Keep a fragment for later

1. Select the structure, including any captions or arrows you want.
2. Choose **Save selection as template**. Give it a name and collection, then **Save**.
3. In its preview, choose an attachment point and **Remember anchor**.

<Screenshot
  src="/manual/custom-template.png"
  alt="A saved Benzofuran template with attachment, Favorite, and editing controls."
/>

Use **Favorite** for quick access. Custom templates also have **Edit**, **Replace from selection**, and **Remove template**. **Undo library change** restores the last library edit.

**Export** and **Import** in Templates share a `.reshiki-templates` collection.

Already have the fragment on the canvas? Select it and choose **Move & attach…** above the canvas.

[Open the benzofuran example](/examples/benzofuran.rsk).
