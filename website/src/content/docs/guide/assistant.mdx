## Describe the drawing

Open **Assistant**, describe what to draw, then **Send**. Try:

> Draw the esterification of acetic acid and ethanol. Put the reactants on the left, ethyl acetate and water on the right, and acid catalyst above the arrow.

The assistant receives the current drawing and can inspect its canvas image. Its proposals use your document's drawing style. You can keep working while it responds.

## Review or apply automatically

| Mode                 | What happens                                           |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Review edits**     | Inspect the proposal, then **Apply** or **Discard** it |
| **Accept all edits** | Applies each validated proposal automatically          |

<Screenshot
  src="/manual/assistant-modes.png"
  alt="Review edits and Accept all edits are available beside the composer."
/>

Use **Replace selection** when replacing part of a drawing. **Undo** restores the previous drawing after an applied edit, in either mode.

Ask for a follow-up such as “Move the conditions above the arrow” or “Keep the existing molecule and add its product.” Use **Stop** to end a running request.

<Screenshot
  src="/manual/assistant-auto.png"
  alt="An automatically applied scheme, including a requested Water caption, remains editable."
/>

## Choose a model

The model menu follows the latest available model until you choose one explicitly. Open the effort menu to choose reasoning effort and service tier. Defaults are **Low** and **Standard**.

<Screenshot
  src="/manual/assistant-model.png"

/>

<Screenshot
  src="/manual/assistant-effort.png"
  alt="Reasoning effort and service tier are separate choices."
/>

The assistant needs a Codex login and internet connection. Check the chemistry and layout before using a generated figure.
