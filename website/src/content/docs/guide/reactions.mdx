## Put the reaction on the page

1. Draw or import the molecules. Put reactants on the left and products on the right.
2. Choose the **arrow** tool. Click to place a fixed-length arrow pointing right, or drag to set its length and direction.
3. Choose **Text** (**T**), click above or below the arrow and type the conditions.
4. Select related objects and use **Properties** to align them.

## Keep conditions on the arrow

Available in Nightly builds; not in ReShiki 0.12.0.

Text you type or drop above or below a straight arrow attaches to it. The text stays centred on its side, and the arrow grows to fit the widest label with a little padding at each end.

- Edit the text: an arrow fitted to the text grows or shrinks with it. An arrow you lengthened by hand keeps its length unless the text outgrows it.
- Drag an end handle to lengthen the arrow. Shortening stops at the label width.
- Move or rotate the arrow: its labels come along.
- Drag the text across the arrow to switch sides, or away from it to detach it.
- Copy, cut or delete the arrow: its conditions go with it.

Each side holds one label; press **Enter** in the text for more lines. Bending the arrow releases its labels. Only `.rsk` files keep the link; other formats keep the text where it is.

## Bend an arrow

Click the arrow tool to reuse the current style. Hold it briefly or click its lower-right triangle to open the palette. Choose **Curved / electron pair**, **Single electron**, or **Bent / elbow** for electron flow and bent arrows. The palette also offers bold, dashed, hollow, and unequal equilibrium arrows.

<Screenshot
  src="/manual/arrow-palette.png"
  alt="The arrow palette includes bent, curved, equilibrium, and retrosynthesis arrows."
/>

Select the arrow. Drag the diamond to bend it, or to move the corner of a bent arrow. A curved arrow also has a square handle on a line from each end. Drag a square to change how the arrow leaves or arrives; both ends stay where they are. Drag a round end handle to move that end. **Properties** provides **Reverse**, **Flip bend**, **Straighten**, and stroke settings. Expand **Arrowhead & markers** for head geometry, dipole crosses, and no-reaction markers.

With an arrow tool active, click an existing arrow to change its style. Repeated clicks with the same style cycle its variants: reverse the direction, change the side of a half-head, or switch the longer line of an unequal equilibrium arrow.

## Point an arrow at atoms

1. Choose **Curved / electron pair** or **Single electron**. **Attach ends to atoms and bonds** in **Properties** is on by default.
2. Click where the arrow starts: an atom, a bond or a lone pair. ReShiki highlights it.
3. Click where the arrow ends. ReShiki adds the arrow in one Undo step.

The arrow follows its targets when they move. To draw a free arrow, Option/Alt-click, drag, or turn off **Attach ends to atoms and bonds**. **Escape** cancels a picked start. To release one end, select the arrow and choose **Detach start** or **Detach end** in **Properties**.

Only `.rsk` files keep the links between an arrow and its targets. Other formats keep the arrow's shape.

## Make the reaction exportable

The drawing can also carry explicit reactant, product, and reagent roles:

1. Select an arrow and open **Properties → Reaction roles…**.
2. Select a molecule, then **+ Assign selected molecules** under its role.
3. Repeat for the remaining molecules.

<Screenshot
  src="/manual/reaction-roles.png"
  alt="The reaction inspector assigns reactant, product, and reagent roles."
/>

Use **RXN** or **Reaction SMILES** to export the chemical reaction. Captions and page layout belong in `.rsk`, SVG, PDF, or PNG instead.

Use **Open** for an RXN file. Use **Import** to paste RXN text or reaction SMILES. Reaction roles do not check reaction balance or predict products.

## Map atoms between reactants and products

Atom maps number the atoms that correspond between reactants and products.

1. Assign reactants and products first. The **Atom maps** section below the roles then shows how many atoms are mapped.
2. To set one map, select one atom, type a number in **Atom map** and choose **Set**. You can also right-click an atom and choose **Edit atom map…**.
3. To pair two atoms, select one reactant atom and one product atom, then choose **Pair selected atoms**.
4. To map the rest, choose **Auto-map**. **Review atom maps** lists each suggested pair; click a row to select that pair in the drawing.
5. Choose **Apply maps** to keep the suggestion, or **Discard**.
6. Choose **Align mapped structures** to turn and move each product so that its mapped atoms line up with the reactants.

**Show maps** shows or hides the map numbers. **Move labels** lets you drag the numbers; press **Escape** when you are done. **Reset label positions** returns them to automatic placement. Each edit is one Undo step.

<Screenshot
  src="/manual/reaction-maps.png"
  alt="Ethanol oxidized to acetaldehyde with map numbers 1, 2 and 3 on both sides, and the Atom maps section of Reaction roles showing six mapped atoms."
/>

**Auto-map** compares structures only; it does not predict a reaction mechanism. It keeps the maps you set and does not map reagents. RXN and reaction SMILES keep the map numbers. Save a `.rsk` file to keep custom label positions too.
