## Set the drawing style

New documents use **JACS / ACS**. Click the style badge to change fonts, bond lengths, and line widths. Review the preview, then **Apply to document**.

The preset menu also includes **Nature**, **RSC**, and **Presentation**. Nature uses 6 pt atom labels with 10.8 pt bonds; RSC uses 7 pt labels with 12.2 pt bonds. Page width and column layout are set separately. See the [Nature structure guide](https://www.nature.com/documents/nr-chemical-structures-guide.pdf) and [RSC author guidelines](https://www.rsc.org/publishing/publish-with-us/publish-a-journal-article/green-chemistry).

**Save style…** and **Load…** reuse settings. **Scale layout with bond length** also resizes existing geometry; leave it off to keep current positions.

## Add artwork

| Add                          | Where                                                                          |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Rectangle, ellipse, brackets | Click to reuse the chosen style; hold or click the corner triangle for presets |
| Line, curve                  | Tools palette                                                                  |
| Chemical symbol or orbital   | Hold the toolbar button or click its corner triangle                           |
| A picture                    | **Import → Picture…**                                                          |

Rectangle presets include rounded, dashed, filled, and square shapes. Ellipses include outline, dashed, filled, and circle presets. Brackets include square brackets, parentheses, and braces, with both sides or either side alone.

Select an object to see its controls in **Properties**. **Molecular properties** shows the calculation scope and formula at the top when atoms are selected. Expand **Atom details**, **Arrange & transform**, or **Grouping & frames** when needed. **Drawing style** remains available below the selection controls.

<Screenshot
  src="/manual/properties.png"
  alt="Molecular properties calculated for the selected cyclopentane, with the other molecule excluded."
/>

Select artwork to change its style in **Properties**. Pictures can be resized, rotated, replaced, or sent behind the drawing. Group a picture and caption to move them together.

## Align objects

Select two or more molecules, arrows, or groups. Right-click the selection and choose **Align middles** to put them on the same horizontal centerline. **Align & distribute…** offers the other edges, centers, and spacing options. The same controls appear first in **Properties → Arrange & transform** for multiple objects. Molecules and groups move together, preserving their internal layout. Undo restores the previous positions.

Right-click an atom, bond, arrow, or selected object for editing commands and its relevant properties. Right-click within an existing selection to keep all its objects selected. **Escape** or a click outside closes the menu. Use the middle mouse button to pan.

## Set the page

Open **View → Page setup…** or **Export → Pages & printing → Page setup…**. Choose paper, orientation, margins, and **Columns / Rows**, then **Apply page setup**.

<Screenshot
  src="/manual/publication-pages.png"
  alt="Two publication pages with a labeled structure selected on the second page."
/>

Use **Fit page** to work on one page or **Fit all pages** to see the document. **View** also contains rulers, grid, and crosshair controls.
