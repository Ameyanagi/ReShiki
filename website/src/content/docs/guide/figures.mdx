## Set the drawing style

New documents use **JACS / ACS**. The style selector in the status bar switches directly between **JACS / ACS**, **Nature**, **RSC**, **Angewandte**, and **SYNLETT / SYNTHESIS**. These presets follow publisher instructions or official stationery; the editor links to each publisher. Direct selection updates matching text/strokes and scales the geometry in one Undo step. [Settings and publisher sources](/developer/journal-drawing-presets/).

Choose **Manage styles…** to edit fonts, label size, bond length and stroke settings with a live sample preview. The fixed footer has **Import**, **Export**, and **Save** icons. Save applies the draft; returning to Properties leaves it unapplied. Export offers a native `.reshiki-style` file or ChemDraw `.cds`. Import accepts those files plus document settings from `.cdx` and `.cdxml`; template artwork and page layout are not imported. **Scale layout with bond length** is off by default in this editor; enable it when existing geometry should resize.

**Dimension units** remembers `pt`, `mm`, or `cm` for bond length, line width, bold width, label margin, and hash spacing. Bare numbers use the displayed unit; add a suffix to override it (`0.5 cm` and `5mm` are equivalent). Use a decimal point, without grouping separators. Label size stays in points and bond spacing stays a percentage. Switching units preserves exact stored sizes and does not edit the drawing or add Undo history. Enter validates the current draft field; Save applies the whole draft. Incomplete or invalid dimensions must be corrected before changing units or saving. A pending draft in another tab retains its original units while an entry is incomplete. The preference persists after cancelling the draft or restarting; other tools retain their labeled units.

## Choose canvas colors

[Compare and download every theme palette and element color →](/guide/color-palettes/)

The theme selector next to it offers **Publication**, **Presentation**, **Pastel**, **Jmol**, **VESTA**, and your saved themes. The **sun/moon** button changes the canvas between white and black paper, independently of journal dimensions. Publication labels elements in neutral ink; the other themes color them. Every theme has a palette of eight hues in a **Strong** row for bonds, text and strokes and a **Tint** row for fills and ring interiors; choose them from the color button in the style bar. **View → Interface → Match canvas / Light / Dark** changes only the surrounding interface.

Choose **Manage themes…** to preview, import, export, edit or delete custom themes. Built-ins are protected. **New theme…** lets you choose a reference palette and adjust lightness and color intensity separately for both modes. The periodic tables and molecule previews update live; use the preview icons for labels, solid tiles, interface tiles or ring-fill examples. **B** toggles bold preview symbols. Import opens a draft for review, Save stores and applies it, and Export writes both palettes without applying them. [Theme file format and contribution guide](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/tree/main/presets/themes).

Styles and themes are separate files. A theme can use RGB or OKLCH colors and includes both light and dark palettes. Saved drawings embed their custom theme, so editing the library does not change previous figures.

To highlight part of a structure, select its atoms or bonds, then choose **Color → Apply to → Highlights** and a **Tint** color. The color stays behind the atom labels and bonds after deselection. Use the crossed-out **Clear highlights** swatch to remove it. Moving, copying and Undo preserve the highlights; ordinary ink colors remain separately editable. Coloring a contracted label keeps existing internal highlight colors and colors its unhighlighted members.

File exports and printing include the visible canvas colors and background by default. ChemDraw files (CDXML, CDX) always get the light canvas's look, without a page rectangle. On a filled shape, structures and text take the ink that reads on the fill, such as black on a light grey card on the dark canvas. Clipboard images and editable ChemDraw copies use a **transparent background** while retaining the visible ink, ring fills and highlights. **Settings → Copy & export** sets another background for copied pictures or exported files, such as white for a journal. See [Background](/developer/figure-export/#background). White ink copied from a dark canvas needs a suitably dark destination. Older files remain readable. Files saved by a newer build may not open in an older one; see [file compatibility](/guide/install/#file-compatibility). [Style, color and contrast details](/developer/drawing-styles/).

## Add artwork

| Add                                     | Where                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rectangle, ellipse, brackets            | Click to reuse the chosen style; hold or click the corner triangle for presets    |
| Text inside a rectangle or ellipse      | Double-click the shape and type; the text stays centred and wraps inside          |
| Line, curve                             | Tools palette                                                                     |
| Pen path (straight and curved segments) | Tools palette → **Pen path**                                                      |
| Chemical symbol or orbital              | Hold the toolbar button or click its corner triangle                              |
| A picture                               | **Import → Choose file…**, or drop the file on the drawing                        |
| Lab equipment (preview)                 | Tools palette → **Equipment**, then pick a piece and a size; click to place       |
| Connect equipment                       | Drag a joint onto another; click a piece twice and drag to pull it out            |
| Clamp and clip equipment                | Drag a clamp onto a neck or a stand's rod; **Add clips to all joints**            |
| Move parts and liquids                  | Select a piece; drag a diamond, click a circle, or use **Properties → Equipment** |
| Keep and share equipment pieces         | Equipment panel → **Libraries**: Import…, Export…, Link lab folder…               |

Rectangle presets include rounded, dashed, filled, and square shapes. Ellipses include outline, dashed, filled, and circle presets. Brackets include square brackets, parentheses, and braces, with both sides or either side alone.

With an orbital tool, **Snap to atoms** in the context row places the orbital's node on the nearest atom, and the drag preview names that atom. Hold **Option/Alt** to place the orbital freely.

With a chemical symbol, click an atom to attach a charge, radical or lone pair. Drag from the atom, or later drag the mark's handle in **Position atom marks**, to move it around the atom in 15° steps just outside the label, skipping steps that would touch a bond; a dashed orbit and dots show the positions. Hold **Option/Alt** or **Shift** to place it freely. Lone pairs always turn across the line from the atom and turn with the molecule when it is rotated or flipped; charges stay upright.

Select an object to see its controls in **Properties**. **Molecular properties** shows the calculation scope and formula at the top when atoms are selected. Expand **Atom details**, **Transform**, or **Grouping & frames** when needed. A selection opens at most one section by itself; sections you open stay open. **Drawing style** remains available below the selection controls.

Select artwork to change its style in **Properties**. Pictures can be resized, rotated, replaced, or sent behind the drawing. Group a picture and caption to move them together.

### Show properties under a molecule

1. Right-click a molecule and choose **Show properties**. **Mol. Wt.** appears centered below the molecule.
2. Choose more rows in the **Show properties** list, or click the pin beside a row in **Molecular properties**. A tick or an inked pin marks the rows shown. Choose a shown row again to hide it.

The caption has one line per property, in the panel's order: `Formula: C6H6O`, `Mol. Wt.: 94.11`, `Exact Mass: 94.0419`, `cLogP: 1.39`, `TPSA: 20.23` and `Anal. Calcd for C6H6O: C, 76.57; H, 6.43`. **Mol. Wt.** follows **View → 2-decimal MW**.

The lines update with every edit to the molecule, in the same Undo step. A salt's counter-ion is a separate molecule. Changing the caption's font, size or color keeps it up to date. Editing its text makes it ordinary text. Deleting the molecule deletes its caption.

CDXML and CDX files keep the caption as ChemDraw linked property text, so ChemDraw updates it too. A caption with **cLogP** or **TPSA** is saved as plain text. ChemDraw files with linked property text open with the link. Text that covers more than one molecule, such as both ions of a salt, opens as plain text. Other formats save the text.

### Draw a pen path

1. Choose **Pen path** in the tools palette and drag the first segment.
2. Click to add a straight segment, or drag to add a curved one.
3. Click the first node to close the path, or choose **Finish**. **New path** starts a separate object.

Each segment is one Undo step; **Escape** cancels the segment you are drawing.

To reshape a path, select it and choose **Edit curve points** in **Properties**. Drag a round node to move it, or a square control to change one direction. Choose a node under **Selected node**; **Next segment** switches the following segment between **Straight** and **Curved**. **Insert after** and **Delete node** add and remove nodes. **Close path** or **Open path** closes or opens the path, and **Continue drawing** extends an open path. Choose **Done** in the context row to finish.

## Point out a detail

Select an atom, a bond, a whole molecule or a text, right-click and choose **Add callout**. Type the callout's text. Its leader stays on what it points at: move the molecule or the text and the leader follows. Deleting the text deletes the leader. ChemDraw gets the callout as a line, a dot and the text.

## Align objects

Select two or more molecules, arrows, or groups. Right-click the selection and choose **Arrange & transform → Align middle** to put them on the same horizontal centerline. The same submenu offers the other edges, centers, and spacing options, as do the **Align** and **Distribute** menus at the right end of the context row. Molecules and groups move together, preserving their internal layout. Undo restores the previous positions.

Right-click an atom, bond, arrow, or selected object for editing commands and its relevant properties. Commands with a keyboard shortcut show it at the right of the menu. Right-click within an existing selection to keep all its objects selected. **Escape** or a click outside closes the menu. Use the middle mouse button to pan.

## Panels and lasting layouts

Select a scheme and choose **Wrap in panel** from the canvas menu. The panel sits behind it, tinted and rounded, and moves with it. **Properties** opens on the **Panel** card: type a title and press Enter, pick a fill, turn **Shadow** on if you want one, then choose **Title bar** or **In border**. **Split columns** and **Split rows** add a divider in the widest gap; each column takes its own title.

To keep molecules or whole reactions in a row, column or grid, select them and choose **Lasting layout…** in **Properties → Grouping & frames**. **Properties → Layout** sets the gutter and alignment. When one item grows, its neighbors move to keep the gutters; bond lengths and reaction roles stay as drawn. **Templates → Figure layouts** has ready-made TOC, comparison and evidence layouts. Their rings and arrows are illustrations that never form a reaction; replace them with your structures. [Panel and layout details](/developer/graphics/).

## Catalytic cycles

Draw the intermediates as structures or text in a rough circle, select them and choose **Make catalytic cycle** from the canvas menu. They line up on a circle with an arc arrow from each to the next. The arrows are worked out from where the parts sit: click a part a second time to select it alone, and dragging it refits every arrow. Drag it well clear of the circle to take it out of the cycle. To add a step, drag a structure or text onto an arc: the arc lights up with **Insert between 1 and 2**, and letting go makes it a new intermediate there. Drag a reagent or product beside a step's arc to add it with a side arrow; a tag such as **Reagent of step 1** says what the drop will do. In **Properties → Catalytic cycle**, choose **Clockwise** or **Counterclockwise**. Choose **Tidy cycle** to space intermediates evenly around the circle and line up reagents and products beside their arcs in one Undo step. Open **Layout** to set **Radius (pt)**, **Gap (pt)** or **Center** text. Open a step to see its reagents, products and controls. **Purpose and properties** opens the parts' purpose and molecular properties. A scheme drawn beside the cycle is still read on its own.

Each step is also a reaction: the step's intermediate and reagents go to the next intermediate and its products. Open a step in **Properties → Catalytic cycle**, or right-click its arc, to **Copy step as reaction SMILES**, **Auto-map step**, **Check step balance** or **Color cycle by origin**. Colors follow the atom maps from one step into the next.

**Make catalytic cycle** first shows the cycle and its order; choose **Make catalytic cycle** or press Enter to make it, or Esc to leave the drawing as it was. A cycle exported as CDXML or CDX opens in ChemDraw as arrows, text and reaction steps, and opens in ReShiki again as a cycle while the file is unchanged. [Cycle details](/developer/graphics/#draw-a-catalytic-cycle), [steps as reactions](/developer/graphics/#use-a-cycles-steps-as-reactions), [ChemDraw and back](/developer/graphics/#open-a-cycle-in-chemdraw-and-back).

## Set the page

Open **View → Page setup…** or **Export → Pages & printing → Page setup…**. Choosing a size or orientation applies it at once. For margins and **Columns / Rows**, type the values, then press Enter or **Apply page setup**. For a table-of-contents graphic, choose **ACS TOC graphic · 8.25 × 4.45 cm** or another graphical-abstract size to see the figure inside its printed box.
