## Set the drawing style

New documents use **JACS / ACS**. The style selector in the status bar switches directly between **JACS / ACS**, **Nature**, **RSC**, **Angewandte**, and **SYNLETT / SYNTHESIS**. These presets follow publisher instructions or official stationery; the editor links to each publisher. Direct selection updates matching text/strokes and scales the geometry in one Undo step. [Settings and publisher sources](/developer/journal-drawing-presets/).

Choose **Manage styles…** to edit fonts, label size, bond length and stroke settings with a live sample preview. The fixed footer has **Import**, **Export**, and **Save** icons. Save applies the draft; returning to Properties leaves it unapplied. Export offers a native `.reshiki-style` file or ChemDraw `.cds`. Import accepts those files plus document settings from `.cdx` and `.cdxml`; template artwork and page layout are not imported. **Scale layout with bond length** is off by default in this editor; enable it when existing geometry should resize.

**Dimension units** remembers `pt`, `mm`, or `cm` for bond length, line width, bold width, label margin, and hash spacing. Bare numbers use the displayed unit; add a suffix to override it (`0.5 cm` and `5mm` are equivalent). Use a decimal point, without grouping separators. Label size stays in points and bond spacing stays a percentage. Switching units preserves exact stored sizes and does not edit the drawing or add Undo history. Enter validates the current draft field; Save applies the whole draft. Incomplete or invalid dimensions must be corrected before changing units or saving. A pending draft in another tab retains its original units while an entry is incomplete. The preference persists after cancelling the draft or restarting; other tools retain their labeled units.

## Choose canvas colors

[Compare and download every theme palette and element color →](/guide/color-palettes/)

The theme selector next to it offers **Publication**, **Presentation**, **Pastel**, **Jmol**, and your saved themes. The **sun/moon** button changes the canvas between white and black paper, independently of journal dimensions. Publication labels elements in neutral ink; the other themes color them. Every theme has a palette of eight hues in a **Strong** row for bonds, text and strokes and a **Tint** row for fills and ring interiors; choose them from the color button in the style bar. **View → Interface → Match canvas / Light / Dark** changes only the surrounding interface.

Choose **Manage themes…** to preview, import, export, edit or delete custom themes. Built-ins are protected. **New theme…** lets you choose a reference palette and adjust lightness and color intensity separately for both modes. The periodic tables and molecule previews update live; use the preview icons for labels, solid tiles, interface tiles or ring-fill examples. **B** toggles bold preview symbols. Import opens a draft for review, Save stores and applies it, and Export writes both palettes without applying them. [Theme file format and contribution guide](https://github.com/Ameyanagi/ReShiki/tree/main/presets/themes).

Styles and themes are separate files. A theme can use RGB or OKLCH colors and includes both light and dark palettes. Saved drawings embed their custom theme, so editing the library does not change previous figures.

To highlight part of a structure, select its atoms or bonds, then choose **Color → Apply to → Highlights** and a **Tint** color. The color stays behind the atom labels and bonds after deselection. Use the crossed-out **Clear highlights** swatch to remove it. Moving, copying and Undo preserve the highlights; ordinary ink colors remain separately editable. Coloring a contracted label keeps existing internal highlight colors and colors its unhighlighted members.

File exports and printing include the visible canvas colors and background. Clipboard images and editable ChemDraw copies use a **transparent background** while retaining the visible ink, ring fills and highlights. White ink copied from a dark canvas needs a suitably dark destination. Older files remain readable. Files saved by a newer build may not open in an older one; see [file compatibility](/guide/install/#file-compatibility). [Style, color and contrast details](/developer/drawing-styles/).

## Add artwork

| Add                                     | Where                                                                          |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Rectangle, ellipse, brackets            | Click to reuse the chosen style; hold or click the corner triangle for presets |
| Line, curve                             | Tools palette                                                                  |
| Pen path (straight and curved segments) | Tools palette → **Pen path**                                                   |
| Chemical symbol or orbital              | Hold the toolbar button or click its corner triangle                           |
| A picture                               | **Import → Choose file…**, or drop the file on the drawing                     |

Rectangle presets include rounded, dashed, filled, and square shapes. Ellipses include outline, dashed, filled, and circle presets. Brackets include square brackets, parentheses, and braces, with both sides or either side alone.

With an orbital tool, **Snap to atoms** in the context row places the orbital's node on the nearest atom, and the drag preview names that atom. Hold **Option/Alt** to place the orbital freely. **Snap to atoms** is available in Nightly builds; not in ReShiki 0.11.0.

Select an object to see its controls in **Properties**. **Molecular properties** shows the calculation scope and formula at the top when atoms are selected. Expand **Atom details**, **Transform**, or **Grouping & frames** when needed. A selection opens at most one section by itself; sections you open stay open. **Drawing style** remains available below the selection controls.

Select artwork to change its style in **Properties**. Pictures can be resized, rotated, replaced, or sent behind the drawing. Group a picture and caption to move them together.

### Draw a pen path

Available in Nightly builds; not in ReShiki 0.11.0.

1. Choose **Pen path** in the tools palette and drag the first segment.
2. Click to add a straight segment, or drag to add a curved one.
3. Click the first node to close the path, or choose **Finish**. **New path** starts a separate object.

Each segment is one Undo step; **Escape** cancels the segment you are drawing.

To reshape a path, select it and choose **Edit curve points** in **Properties**. Drag a round node to move it, or a square control to change one direction. Choose a node under **Selected node**; **Next segment** switches the following segment between **Straight** and **Curved**. **Insert after** and **Delete node** add and remove nodes. **Close path** or **Open path** closes or opens the path, and **Continue drawing** extends an open path. Choose **Done** in the context row to finish.

## Align objects

Select two or more molecules, arrows, or groups. Right-click the selection and choose **Align middles** to put them on the same horizontal centerline. **Align & distribute…** offers the other edges, centers, and spacing options, as do the **Align** and **Distribute** menus at the right end of the context row. Molecules and groups move together, preserving their internal layout. Undo restores the previous positions.

Right-click an atom, bond, arrow, or selected object for editing commands and its relevant properties. Commands with a keyboard shortcut show it at the right of the menu. Right-click within an existing selection to keep all its objects selected. **Escape** or a click outside closes the menu. Use the middle mouse button to pan.

## Set the page

Open **View → Page setup…** or **Export → Pages & printing → Page setup…**. Choose paper, orientation, margins, and **Columns / Rows**, then **Apply page setup**.

<Screenshot
  src="/manual/publication-pages.png"
  alt="Two publication pages with a labeled structure selected on the second page."
/>

Use **Fit page** to work on one page or **Fit all pages** to see the document. **View** also contains rulers, grid, smart guides, and crosshair controls. The grid dots sit on multiples of the bond length.
