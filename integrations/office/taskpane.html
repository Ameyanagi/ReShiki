Connecting to Office…

Draw in ReShiki, copy, then paste here. To edit, select the drawing and choose “Edit selected drawing”.

Keep this pane open while editing. Save in ReShiki, wait for confirmation, then save your Office file.

After a confirmed save, closing ReShiki finishes the edit automatically. An interrupted edit can be ended below; its recovery draft is kept.

In Excel, keep ReShiki objects as plain pictures. Added hyperlinks, assigned macros, shadows, and other picture effects cannot be preserved when editing. Editing requires ExcelApiDesktop 1.1 (Microsoft 365 2509 on Windows or 16.102 on Mac).

About editable drawings

ReShiki stores the native drawing with its preview in this Office file. Images pasted without native ReShiki data cannot be edited. Use Copy editable drawing and Paste editable drawing to transfer between Office files or apps.

Recovery drafts stay on this computer if Office closes or an update fails. The status below each edit shows their location.
